# Hello and welcome to my GitHub! 👋

Hi, I’m Ava—a construction professional who followed her curiosity into the world of data and business intelligence.

My career began in architecture with Al Ahmadiah Construction in the UAE. From there, I moved into civil project management and later worked as a Project Engineer in the oil and gas industry.

Moving to Australia opened a new chapter. I spent nearly two years working in project engineering and coordination within the energy sector, where I became increasingly interested in the information behind project decisions: why problems repeat, where processes slow down, and how better reporting can help teams respond earlier.

Alongside my industry work, I have contributed to three construction research papers exploring AI, machine learning and large language models. This combination of engineering, research and technology eventually encouraged me to pursue the field I had been interested in for years—data and business intelligence.

At the end of 2025, I decided to make that direction the main focus of my career. Since January 2026, I have supported more than five Australian businesses with process analysis, reporting and dashboard development. My work involves identifying operational gaps, investigating business problems and turning scattered information into something decision-makers can actually use.

Today, I build decision-support dashboards using Power BI, SQL, Excel and Python. My construction background also means I understand the operational context behind the numbers—including cost, schedule, quality, risk and project performance.
